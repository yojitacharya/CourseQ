FAQ

Is CourseQ an official university system?

No — CourseQ is not an official school record. You choose what to upload. See our Privacy Policy for FERPA details.

Does CourseQ handle Marketplace payments?

No. Transactions are directly between students. CourseQ is not a broker, escrow, or payment processor.

Can I trust the syllabus parser?

It's a planning tool — always verify against your official syllabus and instructor.

How does CourseQ Pro billing work?

Billed via App Store or Google Play through RevenueCat. Cancel in store settings 24h before renewal.

Can I delete my account?

Yes — use in-app deletion. Some backup logs may be retained per our Privacy Policy.

Why is my username permanent?

Usernames stay fixed after registration for referrals, mentions, and campus identity.
